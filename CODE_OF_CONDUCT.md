# Code of Conduct

LocalScribe follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work, not the person.

Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else
in this project. To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@kelyamany](https://github.com/kelyamany), privately.
Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainer may remove content or block people who break these rules.
